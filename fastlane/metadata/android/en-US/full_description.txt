myFlextime is the easy-to-use time calculator for everyone with a flexitime arrangement.

Do you work flexitime and want to quickly find out how many hours you've worked today?

Or when you can finish work at the earliest? MyFlextime calculates it for you – no ads/no costs

simple, fast, and no fuss.

Here's how it works:

- Enter your start time or use the current time

- Enter your end time or calculate the earliest possible end time

- Enter your breaks and target working hours

- See the results immediately: actual working hours and balance (plus/minus)

Benefits:

- Clear and concise display
- Completely offline – no online account required

- No advertising, no tracking, no hidden costs

- All data stays on your device

- Instant calculation of working hours and flexitime balance

- Calculate the earliest possible end time at the touch of a button

- Manage two jobs simultaneously
- Automatic break deduction is possible and configurable

- Save and export your history

Perfect for public sector employees, office workers, and all jobs with flexitime arrangements.
